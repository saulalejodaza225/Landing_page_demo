## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined synthesis of high-end mechanical engineering, modern editorial layout, and technical rigor tailored for an elite automotive workshop in Bogotá. The brand rejects garage tropes—cluttered layouts, heavy grunge, or neon-heavy aesthetics—in favor of clinical precision, structural integrity, and bespoke craftsmanship.

### Visual Style: Editorial Technical Modernism
- **Architectural Framing:** Structural micro-borders (hairlines in graphite and muted slate) divide complex technical offerings into confident, digestible modules.
- **Engineered Contrast:** Deep graphite-black anchors critical hero elements, diagnostics panels, and certifications, while generous off-white and cool gray canvases allow mechanical photography and typography to breathe.
- **Disciplined Energy:** Action red is strictly rationed. It behaves like an instrument gauge or safety indicator: rare, instantly visible, and reserved exclusively for high-intent conversions and critical status indicators.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet confidence, uncompromising competence, absolute mechanical transparency, and the aura of a Formula 1 pit garage crossed with an architectural studio.

## Colors

The palette models the clean environment of an advanced powertrain laboratory. Backgrounds are clean and airy, punctuated by dense carbon blocks and accented by a singular, high-octane racing red.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#DC2626` - Apex Red):** Used exclusively as a focused activator. Reserved for booking triggers, diagnostic alert states, and primary call-to-action badges. Never used for decorative washes or large background blocks.
- **Secondary (`#12151A` - Carbon Black):** The primary authority tone. Used for dominant headline typography, inverted technical feature blocks, footers, and key diagnostic headers.
- **Tertiary (`#475569` - Industrial Slate):** Mid-tone engineering gray used for technical labels, specifications, telemetry subtitles, borders, and secondary controls.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` / `#F8FAFC`):** Canvas surfaces operate on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and architectural mist (`#F8FAFC`), framed by borders in `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`. Dark inverted panels leverage `#12151A` with interior borders of `#1E293B`.

### Color Roles & Ratios
- **65% Canvas & Neutral Air:** `#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAFC` base layers preserve clarity and modern luxury.
- **25% Carbon & Slate Typography:** `#12151A` text and `#334155` technical metadata construct a readable, grounded framework.
- **8% Technical Graphite Surfaces:** `#1A1D24` surfaces used for service telemetry cards, diagnostic inspection reports, and workshop stats.
- **2% Apex Red:** `#DC2626` strictly directing user eyes to "Agendar Cita", WhatsApp priority dispatch, or active bay statuses.

## Typography

The typographic pairing contrasts mechanical precision with effortless reading fluidity.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines & Technical Data (Space Grotesk):** Provides an architectural, industrial personality with distinct geometric numerals and engineered cuts. Used for titles, KPI statistics, part numbers, and service section anchors.
- **Body & Long-Form Descriptions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Brings contemporary warmth, neutral legibility, and high clarity to mechanical descriptions, warranty policies, and quotation details.
- **Tracking & Micro-Labels:** All metadata badges (`label-md`, `label-sm`) leverage uppercase Space Grotesk with expanded tracking (`0.08em` - `0.12em`) to evoke automotive chassis stamping and aircraft instrumentation panels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column structural grid system that references precision technical blueprints and modern Swiss editorial publications.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a fixed max-width container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` horizontal screen padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen padding.

### Section Cadence & Rhythms
- Layouts alternate between expansive light canvas zones (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`) and high-impact dark technical breakout modules (`#12151A`).
- Content blocks are linked by hairline gridlines (`1px` solid `#E2E8F0` on light surfaces; `1px` solid `#1E293B` on dark surfaces) rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing an assembled mechanical matrix.
- Large editorial vertical gutters (`space-4xl`) frame key hero blocks and service transitions to convey luxury, unhurried craftsmanship, and order.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through structural planar layering and crisp hairlines rather than heavy, diffuse dropshadows. The visual metaphor is that of precision-milled aluminum, carbon-fiber plates, and clean laboratory glass.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC` foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Technical Surfaces & Cards):** Flat white or graphite surfaces bounded by a crisp `1px` stroke (`#E2E8F0` in light mode, `#1E293B` in dark blocks). Ambient depth is minimal: `0 1px 2px rgba(18, 21, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Modules & Popovers):** Elevated cards on hover, service selectors, and dropdown menus utilize a razor-sharp composite shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(18, 21, 26, 0.06), 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(18, 21, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Emergency Sticky CTA):** Floating appointment scheduling trays and image inspection dialogs: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(18, 21, 26, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(18, 21, 26, 0.08)`.
- **Dark Surface Accentuation:** On `#12151A` blocks, depth is reinforced by an inner top edge highlight (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), replicating beveled industrial chassis edges.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft-industrial radius profile (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are intentionally kept tight and controlled (`0.25rem` / 4px base; `0.5rem` / 8px for containers) to echo calibrated steel machinery, laser cuts, and high-performance automotive components. Overly rounded or bubbly forms are strictly prohibited.

### Corner Rules
- **Buttons, Badges, & Inputs:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius. Produces an engineered, tactile feel without the harshness of a raw 90-degree corner.
- **Cards & Diagnostic Modules:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) outer radius with a `1px` structural outline.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Rounded-full is permitted solely for status pips (e.g., active diagnostic bay indicators) and vehicle status tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Apex Red):** `#DC2626` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, semi-bold Space Grotesk. Hover: `#B91C1C` with subtle vertical translation (`translateY(-1px)`). Focus: `2px` ring in `#DC2626` with `2px` white offset. Reserved for "Agendar Diagnóstico" and direct phone booking.
- **Secondary Action (Carbon Structural):** `#12151A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `1px` border in `#1E293B`. Hover: `#1E222A`. Used for service line navigations and technical spec downloads.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Transparent background, `1px` border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`.

### Technical Badges & Status Chips
- **Workshop Status Indicator:** Height `24px`, font size `10px`, uppercase with `0.1em` letter spacing. Contains a `6px` pulsating status dot. Green `#16A34A` for "Bahías Disponibles Hoy", Red `#DC2626` for "Capacidad Crítica".
- **Service Tags:** Monospace feel via Space Grotesk, subtle background `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#475569`.

### Cards & Service Diagnostics
- **Service Feature Card:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1px` solid border (`#E2E8F0`), `8px` corner radius. Header contains an engineered alphanumeric code (e.g., `MOD-01 // ESCANEO COMPUTARIZADO`).
- **Telemetry Metric Card:** Dark theme block (`#12151A`), typography hierarchy displaying large numeric stat (`display-mobile` in `#FFFFFF`), sub-label in `#94A3B8`, with hairline divider rule (`#1E293B`).

### Inputs & Quotation Forms
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** `44px` minimum tap height, `4px` border radius, `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px` border `#CBD5E1`. Inner text in `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Stroke sharpens to `#12151A` with a clean `1px` solid highlight; no bloated, soft-glow outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** `18px x 18px` squares (`2px` radius) with `1.5px` border in `#475569`. Active state transitions to `#12151A` with a pure white geometric checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Vehicle Diagnostic Step Bar:** A 4-stage horizontal pipeline with hairline connectors representing Intake, Computer Scan, Precision Repair, and Dyno Quality Control.
- **Live Bay Telemetry Panel:** A real-time availability indicator showing workshop bay capacity in Bogotá (Usaquén / Calle 134), instilling urgency and mechanical credibility.
